# SwiftPDF Licensing Policy

SwiftPDF is licensed under the **MIT License**.

## Commercial Safety & Dependency Auditing

SwiftPDF maintains a strict dependency policy:
1. **Permissive Only**: Only dependencies licensed under MIT, Apache 2.0, BSD-2/3-Clause, ISC, or Public Domain are permitted.
2. **No Copyleft Contamination**: No GPL, AGPL, or ambiguous copyleft components may be introduced into the closed-source or commercial releases.
3. **Pluggable Commercial SDKs**: Enterprise PDF SDKs (e.g. Apryse, Nutrient) live strictly behind the `PDFEngine` interface adapter and require separate commercial license agreements from their respective vendors.

See `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt` for the complete record of third-party open-source components and licenses.
