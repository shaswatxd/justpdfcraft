# JustPDFCraft

**Local-First, Privacy-Focused PDF, Photo & Student Suite**

## Project Overview

JustPDFCraft is an open-source, client-side web application and workspace engineered for high-performance PDF manipulation, scanned document processing, exam admission photo & signature formatting, and automated handwriting assignment synthesis.

All processing occurs 100% in the user's browser using WebAssembly and Web Workers. No files are uploaded to any external cloud or server.

## Key Domains & Capabilities

1. **Core PDF Viewer & Desk:**
   - Multi-page virtualized desk with Single, Continuous, Spread, and Organize view modes.
   - Non-destructive vector annotation overlays (Highlighter, Pen, Shapes, Text, Signature, Dynamic Stamps).
   - AcroForm builder & direct in-place text editor.
   - Native-feeling text layer with clean transparent selection highlight.

2. **Student & Exam Suite:**
   - Target KB Resizer (e.g. 20–50 KB compression for SSC, UPSC, JEE, NEET).
   - Joint Photo & Signature Combiner.
   - Paper Signature Whitener & Shadow Remover.
   - Name & Date of Photo (DOP) official banner injector.

3. **PDF Utilities & Conversion:**
   - Merge, Split, Rotate, Reorder, Delete pages.
   - Compress PDF, Bates Numbering, Watermarks, Password Protect, Unlocker.
   - Extract Images, Extract Tables to CSV/Markdown, Convert to PDF.
   - Offline OCR powered by Tesseract.js WASM.

4. **Design Philosophy:**
   - Pure AMOLED True Pitch Black (`#000000`) high-contrast theme.
   - Zero AI-generated clichés (clean professional icons, uncluttered dropzones).
   - Dual dedicated workspaces on Home Dashboard: Document (PDF) & Photo (Images).
